€100M REVENUE INDEXING METHODOLOGY

WHY INDEX TO €100M REVENUE?

1. STANDARDIZATION
   • Industry standard practice for value sizing (McKinsey, Bain, BCG all use this)
   • Allows easy comparison across different-sized businesses
   • Simple mental math: €100M base makes percentages intuitive

2. SCALABILITY
   • Linear scaling relationship: Impact (pp) × Revenue = € Impact
   • Easy to scale up or down based on actual revenue
   • Works for any OEM regardless of size

3. TRANSPARENCY
   • Removes need to claim knowledge of exact BMW revenue
   • Focuses discussion on margin improvement %
   • Client can apply to their actual numbers

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

HOW THE INDEXING WORKS

BASE FORMULA:
€ Impact = Margin Points (pp) × Revenue (€M) ÷ 100

EXAMPLE CALCULATIONS:

Scenario: Upper Quartile (Best-in-Class)
• Total Margin Leakage: 3.3pp
• Per €100M revenue: 3.3pp × €100M ÷ 100 = €3.3M
• Step 6 Discount Impact: 2.0pp × €100M ÷ 100 = €2.0M
• Recovery Potential: 2.0pp × €100M ÷ 100 = €2.0M

Scenario: Lower Quartile (Below Average)
• Total Margin Leakage: 8.7pp
• Per €100M revenue: 8.7pp × €100M ÷ 100 = €8.7M
• Step 6 Discount Impact: 4.0pp × €100M ÷ 100 = €4.0M
• Recovery Potential: 4.0pp × €100M ÷ 100 = €4.0M

HOW TO PRESENT THIS IN RFP

governance optimization."

✗ AVOID:

"BMW currently loses €300M to margin leakage"
"BMW has €4M in discount leakage per €100M"
"We will deliver €150M in value to BMW"
